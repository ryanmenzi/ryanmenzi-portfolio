Project Statement ↗

How the Game Works

Pick a card from the shared 5×5 grid. You have one minute to sketch a clue before your team guesses the design process.

Each prompt is something designers do, like sketching or wireframing. Harder cards earn more points.

Visual Identity

The detective gives the box a face. The BC monogram and cursor take up less room on smaller pieces.

Cursor & Keyhole

I cut a keyhole into the familiar shape of a design cursor. Repeated at different angles, it becomes the pattern around the box.

Color Palette

Cream and charcoal carry most of the identity. Bright card borders help players spot the difficulty level at a glance.

Game Guide

The rules unfold in the order players need them. Setup comes first, with scoring explained after the example round.

Gameplay Loop

Setup Shuffle the deck and lay out a 5×5 grid. Split into teams and choose a Lead Designer.

  1. Sketch

    The Lead Designer secretly picks a card and has 60 seconds to draw a clue.

    →

  2. Guess

    The designer reveals the sketch. Teammates work together to identify the design process.

    →

  3. Score

    A correct guess earns 1, 2 or 3 points based on difficulty. A miss earns no points.

↺ Next Round Rotate the Lead Designer and repeat.

Game Over The team with the most points wins.
