I'm Ryan Menzildjian, a student at Binghamton University and multidisciplinary designer interested in branding, marketing, UI/UX, and data visualization.

Before the design starts.

A lot of what I enjoy about design is the part before the designing actually starts. Figuring out what makes something interesting, why people connect with it, and what would make it work better. I’m especially drawn to branding, visual storytelling, and finding ways to turn information into something people actually want to look at.
