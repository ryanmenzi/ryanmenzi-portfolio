About the Work BA Studio • Concept Statement

This fantasy first-person montage is about giving viewers a break from everyday weight and inviting them into a dreamlike world for a moment. I wanted it to feel magical, personal, and open-ended, with no extra characters, so each viewer can imagine their own story while moving through the scene.

Fantasy First-Person Montage

A surreal dreamscape composition blending multi-exposure photo manipulation, atmospheric lighting, and digital matte painting.

Crafted in Photoshop with layered depth, tactile texture work, and an inviting perspective designed to spark personal narratives.
